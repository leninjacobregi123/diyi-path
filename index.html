    Questions

    Good to know

    How does online ordering work?

    Build your list on this page and send it on WhatsApp. We confirm stock and your total, including any delivery charge. Once you pay, your order is confirmed and we keep it ready or deliver it.

    How do I pay?

    By UPI, after we confirm your total on WhatsApp. Your order is confirmed as soon as the payment reaches us.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes — anywhere in Kerala. The charge depends on distance and the size of the order, and we tell you before you pay.

    Is DIYI-PATH the same shop as Kerala Electricals?

    Yes. Same shop, same owner, same place at 6th Mile — with a new name and more to offer.

    Are you open on Sundays?

    We're closed on Sundays. For emergencies, please call us.

    Can contractors send a full material list?

    Of course. Send the list, or a photo of it, on WhatsApp. We'll check everything and keep it ready, or deliver it to your site.
